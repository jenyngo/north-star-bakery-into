About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created around a simple idea: a neighborhood bakery should feel like part of the community. Our bakers begin before sunrise so customers can start their day with bread and pastries made fresh, slowly, and with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on simple ingredients and local grains whenever possible. We believe thoughtful sourcing, patient fermentation, and small-batch baking help us create food that tastes good and supports the community around us.

Meet the Team

Our bakers, pastry team, and front counter staff share a love of handmade food and friendly service. From shaping the morning loaves to helping families plan celebration orders, each team member contributes to the North Star experience.
